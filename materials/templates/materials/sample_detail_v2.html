Methodology: how normalized compositions are derived

Each chart is derived from the raw component measurements of its group and always sums to 100%. The raw values themselves are shown unchanged in the Raw measurements table of each group.

  • All measurements are first converted to weight percent (e.g. g/kg → %, mg/kg → %); component measurements only accept weight-fraction units.
  • If the raw shares sum to more than 100%, all shares are scaled down proportionally.
  • If they sum to less than 100%, the gap is shown as Other.
  • Measurements recorded directly as Other are not used; the remainder is always computed from the measured components.
